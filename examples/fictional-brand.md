# Fictional example: Shelfmark messaging kit
All facts here are invented scenario inputs, not a real business or evidence of performance.

## Brief and sources
S1, fictional brief, 2026-10-16: Shelfmark is a prototype that lets small book clubs store reading lists and meeting notes together. S2, fictional owner preference in this scenario: calm, straightforward voice. Audience: volunteer book-club hosts. Objective: invite feedback on the prototype. Resources: one person, text-only assets, no paid promotion. No customer testimonials, adoption metrics, security review, or time-saving study provided.

## Internal positioning
For volunteer book-club hosts keeping lists and notes in separate places, Shelfmark is a shared reading workspace that groups reading lists with meeting notes. Actual difference versus alternatives remains unvalidated; don't call it unique or best.

Value proposition: Keep your club's reading lists and meeting notes together.

Pillars:
- Reading continuity: stored lists → hosts can refer to prior selections → scenario capability S1; actual usability untested.
- Meeting context: stored notes → a place to revisit past discussions → scenario capability S1; retention or engagement outcomes unproven.

Claim ledger:
- “Reading lists and meeting notes together”: supported only within fictional S1; verify prototype before real use.
- “Saves hosts two hours every week”: unsupported; omit. Unlock with a relevant documented time study.
- “Trusted by hundreds of clubs”: unsupported; omit. Unlock with verified adoption data and permission for any identifiable references.

## Voice
Provisional voice based on S2: friendly, low-key, concrete. Say “reading list” and “meeting notes”; avoid “revolutionize community engagement.” Brand is the selected publisher. No founder autobiography or customer endorsement is invented.

## Draft assets

Headline B: Keep a place for the books you've discussed.

Hero: Your book club's lists and notes, together.
Subhead: Explore a prototype workspace for the books you choose and the conversations you want to revisit.
